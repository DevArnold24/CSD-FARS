Attendance management

CSD FARS helps authorized school personnel manage student attendance through class rosters, assigned classroom cameras, live attendance sessions, recorded corrections, and attendance reports and exports.

Privacy and responsible use

CSD FARS supports authorized school attendance work. It handles student identification and enrollment details, attendance results, staff accounts, and records of attendance corrections. Where facial attendance is used, recognition data supports identifying eligible students during attendance.

Protect school information

Use only your own account and access information needed for your assigned duties. Keep passwords, student information, display links and exported reports private. Do not copy, share or change records without authorization. Sign out on shared devices and clear the student display when it is no longer needed.

Consent and accurate records

Student biometric consent is separate from the staff sign-in acknowledgment. A staff acknowledgment does not provide consent on a student's behalf. Camera recognition can be interrupted or need review; an unrecorded result must not be assumed to mean a student was absent. Authorized corrections retain a record of who made the change and why.

Questions or concerns

Ask the teacher about an attendance discrepancy. Contact the Dean or designated school support personnel about account access, suspected unauthorized use or privacy concerns. Do not include passwords, face data or private camera connection details in a support message.

This public page provides information only. It does not expose student or account records, replace the school's approved privacy notices, or certify legal compliance. Access inside the system remains subject to each user's permissions.
