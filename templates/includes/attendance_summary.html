What these numbers mean

Each entry is one student's attendance for one session. Recorded results include Present, Late, Absent and Excused. Pending means a result still needs to be recorded; it does not mean Absent. During attendance, these students are not yet marked. After attendance ends or is interrupted, check their results.

The final rate is (Present + Late) ÷ (Present + Late + Absent). Excused is separate. A session must be confirmed as ended before its results can contribute to a final rate.
