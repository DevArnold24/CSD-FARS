Camera Configuration

Find your camera, enter its login, then check the picture. You can keep using manual attendance while a camera is being set up.

Power on the camera and connect it to the same router or switch as this system. Then select Find cameras.

Use 0 for the first USB, built-in or virtual webcam; try 1 or 2 for another device. It must be available on the computer running CSD FARS, which may differ from the computer running your browser. No camera password is needed.

Finding a camera only confirms that a device replied. It does not verify the camera login or video stream. Save its details, then use Test saved camera to check for a readable picture.

Search options

The system searches from the computer running CSD FARS. This choice selects the connection for discovery only. Camera video follows Windows network routing; selecting a search connection does not bind the video to it.

Using Wi-Fi and a camera network together

A local camera does not need internet access. Wi-Fi for internet and a wired camera network can both stay connected when their network settings are configured correctly.

If the camera test works only after Wi-Fi is turned off, ask your installer to check for overlapping address ranges and confirm Windows sends camera traffic through the correct connection. This can explain the difference, but does not identify the exact cause by itself.

CSD FARS does not change Windows network settings. Keep internet access available when needed; have your installer correct the routing rather than treating Wi-Fi disconnection as a permanent requirement.

Camera login

Use the camera's existing username and password, which may differ from your CSD FARS login. Ask the person who installed it if you do not know them.

Connection details / manual setup

Choosing a discovered camera fills in its address. You can also enter details provided by your installer here.

The channel path varies by manufacturer and recorder channel. Enter the path from your device's settings. If left blank, port 554 and /Streaming/Channels/101 are used.

Use a full video stream address

A full URL overrides the address and credential fields above. Include credentials in the URL if the stream requires them. The URL is masked because it may contain a password.

What to check next

    Camera availability

    Ready allows new attendance and registration use; it does not confirm a working picture. Inactive, Maintenance and Retired prevent new use and preserve saved attendance history.

    Camera removal

    Review current use, assignments and saved records before removing this camera. Review does not delete anything.

    Review camera removal

      Retiring a camera preserves attendance history and keeps it under Inactive / Retired. Replace its default role and finish any current use or pending camera release before retiring it. Do not delete school records to make a camera removable.

      Room Coverage Board
