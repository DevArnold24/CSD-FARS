    How many cameras can this machine handle?

    The machine's reliable camera limit has not been established. Add cameras gradually during a supervised test with representative people in view. Check recognition, frame delays and attendance controls through a full day at the intended load before accepting that camera count.

    Warnings begin at 85% processor or memory use; 95% is very high load. Camera results older than 2 seconds are flagged. These are monitoring guidelines, not a guarantee of accuracy or a tested camera limit. Brief spikes may come from other applications.

    The viewing-slot limit protects attendance controls. One camera can serve several views; a recognition camera can keep running with no open view. Slots therefore do not set the supported number of recognition cameras.

    Recognition FPS includes tracking between detection passes. Result age starts at server capture and excludes delay inside the camera and browser. This monitor does not change recognition quality, thresholds or active sessions.
